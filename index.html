THE FLOOR IS YOURS
The opportunity

Not a job title. A position on the floor.

Ready to take your warehouse skills further? An All-Rounder is the person the shift leans on, the one who steps in wherever the work is and keeps everything moving.

You are the glue that holds the shift together.

When it gets busy or something goes wrong, you are the one the team looks to. You will not just be doing a job. You will learn the entire Jumbo operation from the inside out, station by station.

FIVE STATIONS ONE ROLE
Why this role

Most people learn one job. You will learn the operation.

Range

Five skill sets, one role

Help desk, reach truck, order control, receiving, housekeeping. You do not specialise into a corner. You build skills that stay with you wherever you go next.

Trust

Be the one they call

When a scanner fails or a truck arrives early, the shift looks for you. That trust is earned fast in this role, and it does not go unnoticed.

Momentum

No two shifts the same

Morning at the docks, afternoon in the pink vest solving problems on the laptop. The day keeps moving, and so do you.

Perspective

See how the whole thing fits together

Standing in every station teaches you why the operation works the way it does: how receiving feeds the floor, how order control protects the customer, where the pressure lands when a shift gets busy. That understanding is what separates someone doing a task from someone who can run a shift.

Backing

A team that has your back

Three team leaders on Shift B and a floor full of colleagues who will show you the ropes station by station. You will not be figuring it out alone.
