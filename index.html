The role

Not a job title. A position on the floor.

Ready to take your warehouse skills further? An All-Rounder is the person the shift leans on, the one who steps in wherever the work is and keeps everything moving.

Why join

Most people learn one job. You learn the operation.

Six skill sets, one role

Help desk, reach truck, ontvangst, AGF, order control, housekeeping. You do not specialise into a corner. You build skills that stay with you wherever you go next.

Be the one they call

When a scanner stops working or the floor does not match the system, the shift looks for you. That trust is earned fast, and it does not go unnoticed.

Nobody works alone here

Ontvangst and reach truck work side by side all day. AGF runs as a pair. The whole operation is built on people covering for each other.
